# Garden Brawls — Delete your account and data

Garden Brawls is developed by **Helioso**. You can delete your player account and its data at any time.

## In the game

Open **Options → Delete account** and confirm.

## By email

If you no longer have the game installed, email **heliosoconsulting@gmail.com** with the subject **"Delete my Garden Brawls data"**. Include your **display name**, and your **player ID** if you know it. We reply within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your anonymous player account (Unity Authentication)
- Your game progress: XP, Tier, online rating (Elo) and Rank, online wins / losses / win streak
- Your display name
- Online match statistics linked to your player ID

Game settings stored only on your phone (sound, music, vibrations, unlocked levels) are removed when you uninstall the game.

Nothing is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep it.

See also the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).
