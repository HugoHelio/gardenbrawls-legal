# Garden Brawls — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 5/11/2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game **Garden Brawls** (the "Game") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The Game is developed and published by **Helioso**, France — "we", "us".

Contact: **heliosoconsulting@gmail.com**

## 1. Summary

- You do not create an account with an email or password. The Game signs you in **anonymously**.
- We store your **game progress** online so it is not lost.
- If you play **online matches**, your **display name** is shown to your opponent.
- **Gameplay statistics** about online matches are collected **only if you agree**.
- There is **no advertising**, **no in-app purchase**, and we **do not sell** your data.

## 2. Data we collect

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Anonymous player ID** (random identifier created by Unity Authentication) | First launch | Identify your game profile without any personal account |
| **Game progress**: XP, Tier, online rating (Elo) and Rank, online wins / losses / win streak | While you play | Save your progress and compute online ratings |
| **Display name** you choose (or a default name such as "Neighbor1234") | When you set it in Options | Show it to your opponent in online matches |
| **Network data** (IP address, connection data) | Online matches only | Connect two players through Unity's relay and lobby services |
| **Online match statistics** (scores, duration, who started, how the match ended, ratings and XP of both players, both anonymous player IDs) | Online matches only, **only with your consent** | Balance the game and improve online play |
| **Technical / crash data** (device model, OS version, engine errors) | Automatically | Detect and fix crashes and performance problems |

Game settings (sound, music, vibrations, unlocked levels) are stored **only on your device**.

We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising ID.

## 3. Service providers

The Game uses **Unity Gaming Services**, provided by Unity Technologies, which processes the data above on our behalf: Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Relay, Lobby and Analytics. Unity's privacy policy: <https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy>.

Data may be processed on servers located outside your country, including in the United States, with the safeguards provided by Unity (such as standard contractual clauses).

## 4. Legal basis (EU / UK users)

- **Contract** (providing the Game you asked for): anonymous ID, game progress, display name, network data for online matches.
- **Consent**: online match statistics. You can refuse or withdraw consent at any time; the Game works the same way.
- **Legitimate interest**: technical and crash data, to keep the Game working.

## 5. How long we keep data

- Game progress and display name: as long as your player profile exists, or until you delete it.
- Online match statistics: kept by Unity Analytics according to its retention settings, and deleted with your profile on request.
- Network data: only for the time needed to run the match.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Change your display name** at any time in **Options**.
- **Delete your account and data**: in **Options → Delete account**, or by emailing us at heliosoconsulting@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Garden Brawls data". Uninstalling the Game alone does **not** delete your online data.
- Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may also ask to **access**, **correct**, **export** or **restrict** your data, or **object** to its use. Contact us at the address above. EU users can also file a complaint with their data protection authority (in France: the CNIL, <https://www.cnil.fr>).

## 7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored by Unity Gaming Services. No system is completely secure, but we only collect what the Game needs.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when new features are added (such as a store or leaderboards). The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Important changes will also be announced in the Game.
